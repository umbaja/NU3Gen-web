Case study

OpenProduct

OpenProduct linked a DNA profile with data on food origin and represented a concept for digital traceability. In 2019 the project was part of the award-winning NU3GEN FOOD concept. On the new site it is deliberately presented as a technology reference / case study, not as an actively available product.

Consumer engagement

Eurospotrebitelia & co-creation

The Eurospotrebitelia civic association focused on protecting and informing consumers, education activities, market and product analyses, and questions of food quality and safety. It organised expert conferences and, in 2020, ran Slovakia's participation in the EIT Food RIS Consumer Engagement Labs, where seniors took part in creating a new food product.

NextGenerationEU

Insect Protein 4.0

Project 09I02-03-V03-00192 is working toward an automated system for rearing and producing alternative protein. The concept covers automated feeding, watering and ventilation, scaling, 3D-printed components, and data collection on welfare, nutrition and environmental impact.

Knowledge transfer

Education & mentoring

Our education activities grew out of teaching nutrigenomics and biological analysis, consumer seminars, and mentoring researchers and early-stage entrepreneurs. The new site brings them together under a single know-how transfer service.
