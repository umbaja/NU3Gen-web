Innovation voucher · CustomDev · 2026

Deep Dive Food Safety

NU3Gen was selected as the expert solution provider for a project prepared by CustomDev under an innovation-voucher scheme. The project connects food safety and personalised nutrition with data normalisation and machine-learning methods.

NU3Gen's role is to prepare a data foundation that allows genetic information from different sources to be compared reliably and then used in the development and validation of analytical models. The project is being prepared under the national project Increasing the Innovation Performance of the Slovak Economy 2, code 401101A334, implemented by the Slovak Innovation and Energy Agency.

Data normalisationDesign and development of a normalisation database for genetic data.
Machine learningPreparation of cleaned and structured training datasets for experimental modelling.
Comparison of approachesComparative evaluation of analytical methods and preparation of methodological recommendations.
NU3Gen's roleSelected expert provider in genetics, data interpretation and personalised nutrition.
NextGenerationEU · project 09I02-03-V03-00192

Insect Protein 4.0

The project is developing an automated and scalable rearing system for alternative-protein production. Automation is intended to reduce labour requirements without making the technology too costly or complex for practical deployment.

The solution covers digitalised feeding, watering, ventilation and other production processes. Components are designed with 3D printing, local manufacture and adaptation to farm size and available inputs in mind.

Rearing automationFeeding, watering, ventilation and monitoring of production processes.
Local manufacture3D-printed components and the ability to scale the technology for a specific deployment site.
Data trailData collection on welfare, animal numbers, nutrition, materials used and environmental impact.
FundingInnovation voucher of €10,766.67 plus VAT of €2,153.33 from the Recovery and Resilience Plan / NextGenerationEU.
EIT Food · 2020

Consumer Engagement Labs

The project involved seniors directly in developing a functional food. Participants were not merely research respondents; they became partners in defining the needs, properties and form of the new product.

NU3Gen contributed its perspective on personalised food safety and the possibility of accounting for genetic predispositions. Combining co-creation, modern production methods and personalisation led toward a food concept better adapted to an individual consumer's needs.

Target groupSeniors involved throughout the food-product co-creation process.
NU3Gen contributionGenetic predispositions, personalised food safety and connecting research with product development.
Related consumer activities

Eurospotrebitelia

The Eurospotrebitelia civic association extended this project experience into consumer protection and information, market and product analyses, and food quality and safety. Between 2014 and 2017 it organised conferences on gastronomy, food labelling, nutrition and consumer topics.

EIT Food RIS Innovation Prize · 2019

NU3GEN FOOD and OpenProduct

In 2019, NU3Gen presented the NU3GEN FOOD concept in the Slovak EIT Food RIS Innovation Prize. The solution combined genetics with personalised nutrition and food-origin traceability. The expert jury selected it as the best Slovak project.

OpenProduct formed the concept's technology layer: linking a raw material's DNA profile with origin information and the product's digital trail. The current website presents it as a technology reference and case study.

Personalised nutritionConnecting genetic predispositions with information that can support nutrition decisions.
DNA traceabilityA verifiable biological profile supplemented with data on product origin and movement.
Result1st place in the Slovak EIT Food RIS Innovation Prize 2019.
Further developmentExperience from the concept informed later food, data and consumer activities.
Performance genetics · 2017

Genetic testing for identifying sporting talent

The project developed from NU3Gen's long-term collaboration with physical-education specialists at Constantine the Philosopher University in Nitra. It explored how genetic information could contribute to assessing an individual's sporting predispositions.

The aim was to create a model that supports the development of sporting potential while identifying risks associated with physical load. The project connected genetic data with expert sports interpretation and became one of the foundations of the consumer-facing sports genetics line.

PotentialIdentifying genetic factors associated with performance, recovery and sporting predispositions.
RiskInterpreting factors that may matter when planning physical load and long-term athlete care.
APVV · 2012

Perceptual genetics and personalised food safety

The founding members of the future NU3Gen team first came together through the project “Perceptual genetics and its application in personalised food safety”. Funded by the Slovak Research and Development Agency, the project investigated how genetically determined differences in perception relate to nutrition and food safety.

The project created the scientific and professional foundation from which NU3Gen s.r.o. later emerged. The company's mission became the transfer of molecular and population genetics into practical solutions for everyday life.

Research questionGenetic differences in perception and their relationship to personalised nutrition and food safety.
Applied impactA foundation for personalised genetic interpretation and the creation of the NU3Gen science startup.
