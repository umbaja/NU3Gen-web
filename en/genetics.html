Human genetics

NU3Gen grew out of work in nutrigenomics, perceptual genetics and personalised genetic interpretation. The consumer DNA-testing line (nutrigenomics and sport genetics), previously available at nu3gen.eu, continues as a separate product under the Genotyp brand — NU3Gen's research arm continues to focus on genomic data, relatedness and experimental questions.
