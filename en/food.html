Food authenticity

Genetic and analytical methods make it possible to verify species composition, raw-material origin and food authenticity. The team's expertise also covers the investigation of adulteration and species identification.

Traceability

OpenProduct began as a concept linking a DNA profile, origin information and a product's digital trail. In 2019, the NU3GEN FOOD / OpenProduct concept won 1st place in the Slovak EIT Food RIS Innovation Prize.

The Eurospotrebitelia and EIT Food Consumer Engagement Labs projects broadened the technological view to include consumer needs, education, conferences and the co-creation of new food products. NU3Food subsequently tried to bring personalisation into everyday food-purchase decisions.
